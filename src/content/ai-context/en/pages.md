## /crystallize/｜Emotional Release with Aroma | CRYSTALLIZE

The entrance to CRYSTALLIZE, the work the captain has carried for seventeen years since 2009. It began with an MLM essential oil that practically fell into her lap and that she couldn't find a way to refuse — a Young Living membership package bought under her name as a thank-you for a website she had built. She didn't know the difference between aroma oils and pure essential oils, and had no interest in aromatherapy. Using them intuitively and undiluted, splashing them onto her skin, she found forgotten memories and buried negative emotions surfacing, as if drawn out by the oils. When she wrote about it in her mixi diary, many readers replied that the same thing had happened to them but they had never been able to say so — because the oils were "said to be" of high vibration, and admitting to negative emotions would mark them as low. The page also covers how the phrase "emotional release with aroma" was born from not knowing, and the difficult years in the Young Living and spiritual industries that led her to withdraw.

## /crystallize-method/01-welcome/｜Welcome

Chapter 1, the reasoning behind the twelve-week course. In the summer of 2009 the captain noticed that essential oils could open emotional release. Before that she carried a strong sense of self-denial and went through a long period of bulimia — eating in quantity and vomiting — unable to manage her relationships with others or herself. Energy healing, past lives, meditation, yoga, spirituality: she tried them all and spent a great deal of time and money without being freed from the difficulty of living. Her position is that most of that difficulty comes not from spiritual causes but from events and experiences between birth and now. Her self-assessment moved from minus forty toward zero step by step. Emotional release takes time; even after the twelve weeks she asks people to keep working with it over three to five years. Accepting the parts of yourself you don't want to see — not as good or bad, but as things that happened for a reason — is the key.

## /crystallize-method/02-approach/｜The Cellular Approach of Essential Oils

Chapter 2. Aromatherapy is generally said to work through scent → brain → body, but with continued use she noticed a different route: skin → cells → memory. In the early days, applying Young Living oils directly, her sleep grew deep and long and she began dreaming heavily of the past — forgotten events and forgotten people, fragments of memory surfacing as if her own past were being drawn out of her. The surfacing was hard; in her case it sometimes lasted more than three months, living each day alongside the parts of herself she had sealed away. As she began giving treatments, she found she was picking up emotions and past memories through touch — from the feet, the back and elsewhere — and, asking clients about it, found they recognized those patterns from the present or past. She came to feel that experience accumulates in the cells, including what is inherited from ancestors. The chapter explains, through the example of parent-child relationships, why a present pattern such as self-blame was built long ago.

## /crystallize-method/03-feelings/｜Knowing Your Own Emotions

Chapter 3. It opens with a tree: given light, water and room, it grows straight; against obstacles it bends and warps toward the sun — and the human heart works the same way under its conditions. A child treated with respect grows up believing they matter; a child treated with denial grows up denying themselves. During the course, an hour a day (or five days a week) is set aside as time spent with yourself, cutting off television and phone to listen inward. Holding that most emotional problems originate in the relationship with one's parents, the first two to three weeks are spent interviewing the participant about their family environment, their parents, the era and the household, and choosing a focus — one parent at a time. This is the hardest stretch of the course. The chapter also covers the benefits of writing, which came out of a program built to run entirely by email while she was living in Cuba, buying WiFi cards for four dollars an hour by queuing at the telephone office.

## /crystallize-method/04-collapse/｜Let's Collapse — Thinking from a Different Perspective

Chapter 4. What gets collapsed are the values, ways of thinking and fixed beliefs that constrain a person. Because the head can understand something the heart and body won't follow, the work runs on two tracks: releasing emotion in the cells with the oils, while breaking down the walls in the head. "Common sense" and "obvious" are shown to be products of environment and experience — the captain has traveled widely and lived in at least three entirely different countries, and learned there how narrow her own frame had been. When a belief breaks there is shock, and sometimes fear that the self will vanish with it; but after the third or fourth time she learned that reconstruction always follows, and that the view afterward is wider than before. The chapter is explicit that CRYSTALLIZE's emotional release is not healing — it is destruction and letting go — and that she will say hard things when a belief refuses to move.

## /crystallize-method/05-usage/｜How to Use Essential Oils

Chapter 5, the practical part. The approach is not scent but getting the oil into the body — stimulating and washing the cells — and it uses far more oil than ordinary aromatherapy (generally diluted two to five times in carrier oil). Preparation begins with "Inner Strength": before sleep, six drops in the palms, holding each sole quietly for five to ten minutes, then fifteen to twenty drops total over the neck, throat, shoulders, back, sides, chest, solar plexus and abdomen, then rest. Three times a week for one to three weeks. Oils loosen held tension, so sleepy days may follow — sleep as long as you need, since the brain processes information during sleep. Each day the participant reports how it felt, any shift in mood, any old memory that returned, and any dreams; the weekly menu is built from those reports. The chapter also covers the inward-looking work of sensing yourself.

## /crystallize-method/06-rose/｜How to Use Rose

Chapter 6. Rose is said to carry the highest vibration of the essential oils, and it did the finest work in emotional release; CRYSTALLIZE uses organic Bulgarian Damask rose otto. "Angelic" is a blend of rose otto and frankincense carterii in carrier oil. After the release work, the negative energy freed from the cells is cleared with rose: fifteen to twenty drops of Angelic mixed into salt as a bath salt, submerging the head from time to time; then the same amount diluted eight to ten times over the whole body; then one drop of rose in the palm on the heart or solar plexus, another on the matching point on the back, holding yourself between both hands and sensing whether you are actually in there. People who absorb others easily, or who are very busy, sometimes find no one home. The chapter connects a solid self-axis to clearer boundaries with others, and gives a protection blend (one drop rose, two frankincense, diluted five to six times).

## /crystallize-method/07-rewrite/｜Overwriting with New Experience

Chapter 7, the last. Once the old negative beliefs are let go, the work of overwriting with new experience begins. The twelve weeks can only sort through some of the past beliefs — far enough to see the door, not through it. A belief is described as a room: break one open and you may see an ocean you couldn't see before, and once you see it you start wanting to lie on the sand, to swim, to fish, to watch the sun go down. Go alone; you don't need to swim. Being spoken to by someone unpleasant is not failure but experience, as long as your life isn't in danger. Just as negative thinking was imprinted by repetition, new patterns can be imprinted by repeated new experience — and that takes action. If in five or ten years you find yourself puzzled at how the old you ever thought that way, the work has succeeded.
